Government & Healthcare Ecosystem Integrations

Engineered for seamless interoperability with the Ministry of Home Affairs (MHA), National Health Authority (NHA), and State Forensic Science Laboratories across all 700+ Indian districts.

Coming Soon · Phase 2

1. National ICJS & Police CCTNS Interoperability Pipeline

Directly transmits cryptographically sealed MLCs to the Inter-operable Criminal Justice System (ICJS) and State Police Crime and Criminal Tracking Network & Systems (CCTNS) without manual dispatch.

Statutory Impact & Use Case
  • ✓ Eliminates lost paper certificates between hospital casualties and police stations.
  • ✓ Auto-links sealed medical records to police FIRs and e-Courts chargesheet filing.
  • ✓ Complies with digital evidence transfer under Section 193 BNSS 2023.
Target Agency: MHA / NCRB / Police
In Development · ABDM M3

2. ABDM & NIC e-Hospital HL7/FHIR Microservice

Plug-and-play adapter for the National Health Authority's Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission (ABDM) and NIC's e-Hospital EHR suite across 25,000+ government hospitals.

Statutory Impact & Use Case
  • ✓ Zero-workflow disruption: hospitals plug Pramaan into their existing e-Hospital instance.
  • ✓ FHIR R4 standard payload mapping with ABHA Health ID token verification.
  • ✓ Maintains National Health Facility Registry (HFR) cryptographic signatures.
Target Agency: NHA / MoHFW / NIC
Coming Soon · Hardware Pilot

3. State FSL Biological Specimen & Viscera Barcode Chain

Generates serialized, tamper-evident 2D cryptographic barcodes for physical blood, viscera, and DNA specimen envelopes sent to State Forensic Science Laboratories.

Statutory Impact & Use Case
  • ✓ Physical specimen packaging container links 1:1 with digital blockchain block.
  • ✓ Guarantees chain-of-custody from emergency casualty ward to forensic lab bench.
  • ✓ Precludes defense claims of sample contamination or envelope swapping.
Target Agency: State Forensic Science Labs
Powered by Sarvam AI · Beta

4. Sarvam AI Indic Voice Dictation & Structuring Agent

Allows emergency doctors to dictate casualty injury findings in regional Indian languages (Hindi, Marathi, Tamil) and auto-structures them into court-admissible legal MLC summaries.

Statutory Impact & Use Case
  • ✓ Zero typing in high-stress trauma wards: voice dictation to cryptographic seal in < 30s.
  • ✓ Transforms spoken colloquial injury terms into standardized medical nomenclature.
  • ✓ Generates bilingual (English + Regional) certificates automatically.
Target: Casualty Trauma Surgeons
