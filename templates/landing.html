THE SYSTEMIC PROBLEM

Why Criminal Justice Collapses in Indian Medico-Legal Cases

In India, over 70% of assault, accident, and POCSO trials rely heavily on medical evidence. Yet, the current manual paper workflow creates devastating vulnerabilities where evidence gets altered, delayed, or lost.

⚠️

1. The "Vulnerability Window"

Physical paper registers are written by hand, stored in casualty cupboards, and physically transported weeks later. This creates a multi-day window where medical reports get altered or pages swapped under bribery.

⏳

2. The 47-Day Forensic Delay

Forensic examination reports and biological viscera results frequently get delayed beyond statutory 60-day POCSO or 90-day chargesheet limits, putting criminal custody and judicial timelines at critical risk.

⚖️

3. No Zero-Trust Verification

Judges and investigating police officers currently have no machine-verifiable tool in the courtroom to prove whether a scanned photocopy presented in trial matches the exact bytes signed by the examining doctor.

Can anyone test this independently right now on their phone? ▼

Yes. Open the Live Verifier on /verify/2 or scan the QR code with your phone camera. Your browser will compute the SHA-256 hash client-side with zero server round-trips.
